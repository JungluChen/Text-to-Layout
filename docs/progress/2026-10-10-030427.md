## Reproduce, diagnose, fix

Previous report 2026-10-09-210224 retained repeated actual scqubits spectra
showing a rounded-flux-constant conversion discrepancy. Four new regressions
at EJ/h=15 and 30 GHz failed twice unchanged in both product and legacy paths.
They invert EJ/h=Ic/(4*pi*e), allowing 1e-14 relative arithmetic roundoff.
This is a units-identity test, not a fitted physical accuracy threshold.

Both paths now compute Phi0=h/(2e), from exact SI h=6.62607015e-34 J s and
e=1.602176634e-19 C. The legacy extraction constant also feeds other legacy
callers: this intentionally corrects that common constant, without changing
formulas or public interfaces. Product result lineage records constants and
Phi0 definition. The existing product/legacy regression remains at its original
1e-12 relative tolerance. Historical artifacts were not regenerated or relabeled.
Convergence/reference remain NOT_EVALUATED; executed is not validated.

Twelve targeted tests passed twice after the fix. The original two-case probe
was repeated twice with cutoffs 15/25/35 and six energy levels at ng=0; audit
JSONs were identical. Largest Mathieu comparison difference across the inspected
levels/cutoffs fell from 3.378520966 Hz to 0.002292633 Hz. At cutoff 35 the
maximum is 0.000179291 Hz. These observations verify the conversion correction;
